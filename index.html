Short Bio

My name is Ming Zhu, from University of Chinese Academy of Sciences. My research interests include deep reinforcement learning, machine learning, non-convex optimization, and quantum computing.

I created several open-source projects, such as RLSolver, ElegantRL, FinRL, FinRL-Meta, and FinGPT. I am a Co-Founder of AI4Finance foundation and SecureFinAI foundation . I am also the key organizer of the RLSolver contests .

My research papers have been published in top AI journals and conferences, including NeurIPS, IJCAI, TNNLS, TITS, Springer Nature, etc.
