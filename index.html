Short Bio

My name is Ming Zhu, is from University of Chinese Academy of Sciences, Beijing, China. My research interests include deep reinforcement learning, machine learning, and non-convex optimization.

I created several open-source projects, such as RLSolver, ElegantRL, FinRL, FinRL-Meta, and FinGPT. I am a Co-Founder of AI4Finance foundation and SecureFinAI foundation. I am also an organizer of the RLSolver contest.

My research papers have been published in top AI journals and conferences, including NeurIPS, IJCAI, TNNLS, TITS, Springer Nature, etc.
